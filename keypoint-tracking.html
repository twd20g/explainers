The zoomed exemplar is locked onto one corner by forward matching — each frame it re-finds the nearest descriptor to the previous exemplar, so it stays put instead of jumping; the arrow keys step it to neighbouring keypoints (by strength rank, so the tour hops around a little). FAST flags a pixel as a corner if a contiguous arc of ≥9 pixels on a 16-pixel ring are all brighter than the centre+t or all darker than centre−t; the inset zooms one candidate and colours its ring. BRIEF describes each corner by 256 fixed brightness comparisons in its (smoothed) patch, giving a 256-bit code; matches are the smallest Hamming distance, kept only when the best is clearly better than the second-best (Lowe ratio). This BRIEF is not rotation-invariant, so it tracks translation and gentle motion best. All processing is local to your browser.
