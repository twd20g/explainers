Frequency-domain panels show the log-magnitude spectrum, fft-shifted so the DC term sits at the centre and low frequencies are central. Multiplying two spectra (the product panel) is the same operation as sliding the kernel across the image. Because the FFT assumes periodicity, this is a circular convolution — the interior matches a spatial convolution exactly; only the wrap-around border differs. The edge window tapers the crop toward the mean grey at its border (a Tukey window): without it, the hard crop edge leaks each spectral peak into a horizontal + vertical cross, since the transform of a rectangular window is a 2-D sinc. Toggle it off to see the leakage. Remove DC hides the bright central term so the display keys its brightness off the real peaks (spectrum-only — the reconstructed output keeps its mean). Denoise applies a light Gaussian to the frame first, which suppresses sensor/JPEG aliasing before it reaches the transform. All computation is local to your browser.
